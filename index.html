🥐 Croissant

A brief history 13th century: The croissant's ancestor, the kipferl, a crescent-shaped pastry, appeared in Austria. 1683 (popular legend): It is said that Viennese bakers created the crescent-shaped pastry to celebrate the defeat of the Ottoman siege of Vienna. However, this story is disputed. Late 18th century: The pastry was introduced to France, supposedly by Austrian-born Marie Antoinette or other Viennese influences. 19th century: French bakers developed the buttery, flaky, laminated pastry we recognise today. The croissant has Austrian roots but became the iconic French pastry we know and love today!
